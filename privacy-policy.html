SINKING — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · applies to the SINKING apps for Android and iOS/iPadOS

In short: the game itself collects nothing — no accounts, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and your saves never leave your device. The app is free and shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on its menu screens. To show and measure those ads, Google's advertising software in the app collects some device and usage information, as described below. You can limit this at any time.

Who we are

SINKING ("the app") is published by SINKING Dev. Contact: eurikamonica@gmail.com.

Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not run servers for the app, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information or gameplay data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When ads are requested, Google may automatically collect and process:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (approximate, not precise);
  • device and advertising identifiers — the Android advertising ID and app set ID, or on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and app-scoped identifiers;
  • interactions such as app launches, taps and which ads were shown or viewed;
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash logs of the ad software.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and personalise ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud and abuse. It is sent encrypted (TLS). Google's processing is governed by Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The only ad is a banner on the menu screens; it is limited to content suitable for teens and is never shown while a game is running.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ads & privacy → Privacy & ad choices.
  • iOS tracking: the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • US state privacy laws: where Google's US state regulations message is enabled, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for ads through the same Privacy & ad choices button.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, saved games, replays and custom ships are stored only inside the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you use your device's own backup (Android Backup to your Google account, or iCloud/device backup on iOS), the operating system may include this game data in your personal backup; those backups are handled by Google or Apple and we have no access to them.

Permissions

Android: internet and network state (for ads), the advertising ID (for ads), and vibration for optional haptic feedback. iOS/iPadOS: the App Tracking Transparency prompt described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

App stores

Google Play and the App Store may process information about your download under their own privacy policies. We receive only the anonymous, aggregated statistics that the stores provide to all developers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app with a new effective date. The app shows this policy under Settings → Ads & privacy → Privacy policy.

《沉船》隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日 · 适用于 Android 与 iOS/iPadOS 版《沉船》（SINKING）

简而言之：游戏本身不收集任何数据——没有账号、没有统计分析、没有内购，存档只保存在你的设备上。本应用免费，只在菜单界面显示 Google AdMob 横幅广告。为展示和统计这些广告，应用内的 Google 广告软件会收集部分设备和使用信息（见下文），你可以随时加以限制。

开发者

本应用由 SINKING Dev 发布。联系方式：eurikamonica@gmail.com。

我们自己收集的信息

无。我们没有为本应用运行任何服务器，不收集、不接收、不出售、不共享任何个人信息或游戏数据。

广告（Google AdMob）

本应用内含 Google 移动广告 SDK。请求广告时，Google 可能自动收集并处理：

  • 设备的 IP 地址（可用于估算大致位置，非精确位置）；
  • 设备与广告标识符——Android 广告 ID 和应用集 ID；iOS 上仅在你允许跟踪时使用广告标识符（IDFA），以及应用范围内的标识符；
  • 互动信息，例如应用启动、点击以及展示或观看了哪些广告；
  • 诊断信息，例如应用启动时间、卡顿率、耗电量以及广告软件的崩溃日志。

Google 使用这些信息投放、衡量和个性化广告，进行统计分析并防止欺诈和滥用，传输时经过加密（TLS）。相关处理受 Google 政策约束：Google 隐私权政策、Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。

唯一的广告是菜单界面的横幅，内容限制为适合青少年的级别，游戏进行中不会显示。

你的选择

  • 同意（欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士及其他法律要求的地区）：在展示个性化广告前，应用会通过 Google 的同意表单征求你的同意。你可以随时在游戏的「设置 → 广告与隐私 → 隐私与广告选项」中更改。
  • iOS 跟踪：应用会通过 Apple 的“App 跟踪透明度”弹窗请求跟踪许可；若你拒绝，则不会使用广告标识符。你可以在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中更改。
  • Android 广告 ID：可在 Android 设置（隐私 → 广告，或 Google → 广告）中重置或删除。
  • 美国各州隐私法：如启用了 Google 的美国州级法规消息，你可以通过同一个「隐私与广告选项」按钮选择退出为广告目的“出售”或“共享”个人信息。

保存在你设备上的数据

设置、存档、录像和自制船只只保存在本应用在你设备上的私有存储中，绝不会发送给我们或任何第三方，卸载应用即会删除。若你开启了系统备份（Android 备份到你的 Google 账号，或 iOS 的 iCloud/设备备份），系统可能会把这些游戏数据包含在你个人的备份中；这些备份由 Google 或 Apple 处理，我们无法访问。

权限

Android：网络与网络状态（用于广告）、广告 ID（用于广告），以及用于可选触感反馈的振动权限。iOS/iPadOS：上文所述的“App 跟踪透明度”弹窗。

儿童

本应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

应用商店

Google Play 与 App Store 可能依据其各自的隐私政策处理与你下载相关的信息。我们只会收到商店向所有开发者提供的匿名汇总统计。

变更

如本政策有变更，新版本将发布在本页面和应用内，并更新生效日期。应用内可在「设置 → 广告与隐私 → 隐私政策」查看本政策。
